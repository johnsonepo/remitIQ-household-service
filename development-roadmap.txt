==================================================
Development Principles
==================================================

1. Database per service.
2. Never share databases.
3. Communicate only through APIs.
4. Authentication belongs to Household Service.
5. Market Service never owns users.
6. Write tests with every feature.
7. Document important decisions.
8. Keep services independent.
9. Prefer simple maintainable solutions.
10. Build production-ready features incrementally.
